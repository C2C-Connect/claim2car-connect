# Research backlog

Source: `claim2car-faos-v1` `docs/research-backlog.md` at `38bae9d763308732a9a6c3401d5791caef4f1e83`, plus the deferred rows in `docs/phase-c-d-requirements.md`. Every entry is unvalidated. None of them may route a lead, price a lead, or replace human review.

Promotion of any item needs an owner, a protocol, a dataset, and recorded results. Documentation is not that promotion.

## DARCI

Keep inputs, units, provenance, coordinates, assumptions, uncertainty, model version, and where the method applies. Promotion needs a reviewed method, controlled and field comparisons, criteria declared beforehand, reproducibility, and a written failure analysis. A missing input stays unavailable. Do not invent speed.

## Ensemble weights

A 55/45 physics-and-perception blend is a candidate version, not an established consensus and not a pilot rule. It needs shared targets, calibration, a baseline, and a rule for disagreement. No automatic structural verdict in the pilot.

## Edge runtime

A WASM build is a candidate. A sub-50 ms claim has to name the operation, the device, cold and warm runs, preprocessing, and tail latency. A per-assessment cost target has to name what is included. Compilation does not prove physical accuracy.

## Manifest structure

SHA-256 binding is the integrity direction already in the product contract. A Merkle layout is research until encoding, leaf order, empty and odd leaves, versioning, and trusted-root storage are specified and negative tests exist for alteration, omission, duplication, and reordering. Integrity is not authenticity and not admissibility. Ledger anchoring stays optional research. Blockchain is out.

## Hardware and quality

An M4 iPad Pro is the intended later device, not the selected M1 device. Depth has to be shown on that hardware before it is required. Laplacian variance 160 is a configurable starting point, not a passed calibration. Offline recovery has to be shown on a physical device.

## Structural override and carrier export

Frame or chassis yield, and any numeric structural override, stay hypotheses until the formula and its units are defined. Carrier export mappings stay reserved until a named schema and a partner check exist. Neither one is an operational total-loss authority.
