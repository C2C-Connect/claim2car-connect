03 · CONDITION RECORD

Document vehicle condition

16 VIEWS

Capture the 16 target views where safe and accessible. You can add observations about visible damage. Missing, unsafe, or inaccessible views remain missing; do not infer hidden damage from exterior photographs.

Safety: Do not enter traffic, crawl beneath an unsupported vehicle, open hazardous compartments, or delay emergency response to complete these views. Mark unavailable views and seek qualified inspection where necessary.

Visible damage observations

Record only what you can actually see. These are user-entered observations, not AI findings or a professional repair estimate.
