Identify the vehicle

VIN format and check digit are validated. The server stores a keyed reference, not the raw VIN.

4

Capture original evidence

Use the device camera or choose an image. Original bytes are uploaded without resizing or recompression. Retaking a slot supersedes its prior artifact.

Roadside safety comes first. Never enter traffic or delay emergency response, police direction, stabilization, loading, or scene clearance to complete a capture. If a required angle is unsafe, stop and leave the case unsealed. This initial UI cannot yet record a server-side skip or abort event.
